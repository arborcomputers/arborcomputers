Our Team at Arbor Computers

The people behind reliable technology support bring hands-on troubleshooting, careful planning, and a commitment to clear communication. We work with dental practices, businesses, and home computer users throughout Southeast Michigan.

Technology expertise with a personal approach

Arbor Computers has served Ann Arbor and Southeast Michigan for more than two decades. Our approach starts by understanding what the customer needs, investigating the issue, and discussing a practical path forward. Whether supporting one computer or an office network, our team aims to make technical decisions understandable.

Dental and medical practice support

Clinical offices depend on practice-management software, imaging workstations, networks, servers, secure access, and backups. Arbor Computers helps investigate and maintain those supporting systems, including coordinating specialized equipment issues with the appropriate vendors. Technology safeguards support healthcare responsibilities but do not automatically establish HIPAA compliance.

Business IT and managed support

Our work includes business workstations, server and network administration, Wi-Fi, Microsoft 365 email, cloud access, security maintenance, and backup planning. Services and availability depend on the actual environment and agreed support scope.

PC and Mac repair

Our in-shop and onsite technology work includes diagnostics, software troubleshooting, component repairs, compatible upgrades, and assistance with recovering important files where feasible. We explain the condition of equipment and available choices before proceeding.

Security, backup and continuity

Account controls, endpoint protection, software updates, careful access permissions, and workable recovery procedures help reduce technology risk. We work with customers to identify what matters most and to plan support appropriate for their systems.

How we work with customers

Clear communication and professional attention matter as much as the technology itself. We discuss findings, work with relevant suppliers when necessary, and help customers understand service options and next steps. Our goal is practical, dependable support rather than one-size-fits-all promises.
