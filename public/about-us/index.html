Ann Arbor IT support & computer services

For 22 years, Arbor Computers has delivered Dental IT, Business IT, managed IT, cybersecurity, PC and Mac repair and custom-built computers to hundreds of practices, businesses and home users across Southeast Michigan. One local team that listens first, explains clearly and delivers support you can depend on.

“

For 22 years, our work has rested on one simple belief: technology should help people do their best work, never stand in their way.

In that time, we have supported hundreds of dental practices, businesses and home users across Southeast Michigan. The technology has changed many times. The way we work has not. We listen first, explain what we find in plain language, and recommend only what truly makes sense for you.

Most of our clients come to us through referrals and long-standing relationships, and I never take that trust for granted. Whether it is a single laptop at our counter or the network behind a busy practice, every job receives the same care, the same honesty and the same accountability.

Thank you for considering Arbor Computers. My team and I look forward to getting IT done for you.
