Dental IT questions

See Dental IT services →
Why does a dental practice need specialized IT support?

Scheduling, patient records, digital imaging, and treatment planning depend on reliable, connected technology. A problem in one system can affect the entire practice. Arbor Computers provides dental IT support that helps these systems work together, protects sensitive information, and reduces avoidable disruptions to patient care.

Which dental software and imaging systems do you support?

We support a wide range of dental practice management and imaging platforms. Our team assists with setup, upgrades, troubleshooting, and system compatibility. Share your practice’s software and equipment details with us so we can confirm the support available for your specific setup.

Will new computers and equipment work with our existing dental software?

Compatibility depends on your software version, operating system, imaging equipment, and network configuration. Proposed equipment should meet the relevant vendors’ requirements. Our dental IT services include server and workstation setup, software upgrades, and troubleshooting to help your practice make informed technology decisions.

How can IT support help with HIPAA compliance?

We support compliance efforts through network and email protection, IT assessments, and backup and recovery planning. HIPAA compliance also involves risk management, staff training, documented policies, and appropriate physical safeguards. Technology contributes to those responsibilities, but no individual product or IT service establishes compliance on its own.

How should a dental practice prepare for a server failure or data loss?

A sound recovery strategy combines protected backups, restoration testing, and documented procedures. The plan should identify critical patient information, essential systems, recovery responsibilities, and how the practice will maintain necessary operations during an outage. Arbor Computers provides dental backup and disaster recovery services to support this preparation.

Can you help with technology planning for a new or expanding dental office?

Yes. Our services include network infrastructure, server and workstation setup, dental software support, and technology planning. These elements should be considered together so the office’s systems support its clinical and administrative requirements. We help identify technology needs associated with your practice’s space, equipment, and growth.

What is managed IT support?

Managed IT provides ongoing oversight and support for the technology your business relies on. Arbor Computers offers workstation and server monitoring, technical assistance, network management, and related IT services. The goal is to maintain dependable systems, address technical problems, and help employees use their technology effectively.

Do you provide both onsite and remote IT support?

Yes. We provide onsite and remote assistance for business technology, including computers, servers, networks, printers, and Wi-Fi. The appropriate approach depends on the problem and the equipment involved. How can we support remote employees securely?

Secure remote work combines appropriate access permissions, maintained devices, multifactor authentication, and clear procedures for handling company information. Can you support our servers and business network?

What types of computer problems can you repair?

We work on PCs and Macs with issues including startup failures, charging problems, damaged screens, malware infections, and liquid damage. We also provide hardware upgrades and data recovery assistance. Repair options depend on the device’s condition, the underlying fault, and the availability of compatible components.

Should I repair or replace my computer?

The right choice depends on the computer’s condition, software requirements, and ability to meet your needs. A repair or targeted upgrade may be appropriate when the remaining hardware is suitable. We assess systems and recommend upgrade options, helping you understand whether improving your current computer is practical.

Will my files be preserved during a repair?

Data preservation depends on the problem and the work required. Many repairs leave personal files unaffected, but storage failure and some software repairs can put information at risk. Can you recover files from a computer that no longer works?

Can you improve a slow computer without replacing it?

In many cases, performance problems can be addressed through troubleshooting, system cleanup, or compatible hardware upgrades. The appropriate solution depends on what is limiting the computer and how you use it. Custom Build questions

See Custom Builds →
Who can benefit from a custom-built PC?

A custom PC can support gaming, video editing, design, CAD, and other demanding applications. Arbor Computers recommends builds around how you intend to use the system. This approach allows hardware choices to reflect your software requirements, performance goals, storage needs, and preferred setup.

How do you choose components for a custom PC?

Component selection starts with the applications and workloads the computer needs to handle. The processor, graphics card, memory, and storage must work with a compatible motherboard, suitable power supply, and effective cooling. We help identify compatible hardware and consider case space and airflow as part of the configuration.

Can I customize the appearance and layout of my PC?

Yes. A custom build can reflect your preferences for case design, lighting, component layout, and cooling. Appearance should be balanced with practical requirements such as airflow, hardware clearance, and accessibility. Discussing these preferences helps shape a system that suits both your workspace and the way you use it.

Can a custom PC be upgraded later?

Many custom PCs allow upgrades to memory, storage, graphics, and other components. Available options depend on motherboard compatibility, power capacity, cooling, and case space. Considering these factors during component selection can provide flexibility, although no configuration can guarantee support for every future hardware release.

Can you help with a PC I have already started building?

Yes. Our team assists customers who need help with an existing build, including selecting compatible hardware and improving component fit and airflow. Bring your component list and explain the issue or goal. This gives us a useful starting point for discussing the assistance your build needs.

What maintenance does a custom PC need?

A custom PC benefits from dust removal, appropriate software and driver updates, and checks for cooling or performance problems. Maintenance should reflect the components and the environment in which the system operates. Arbor Computers also provides troubleshooting and upgrade assistance when your computer’s needs change.

Cybersecurity questions

See Cybersecurity services →
Does a small business need cybersecurity protection?

Yes. Business accounts, customer information, and connected systems require protection regardless of company size. A practical security approach should reflect the information you hold and the technology you depend on. It should address prevention, recognition of suspicious activity, and preparation for recovering from an incident.

Is antivirus software enough to protect a business?

Antivirus is one part of a broader security approach. Account protection, software maintenance, network safeguards, backups, and employee awareness address risks that antivirus alone cannot cover. Arbor Computers’ cybersecurity services include endpoint protection, antivirus, cloud security, and monitoring to help businesses strengthen their defenses.

How can a business reduce the risk of ransomware?

Use multiple safeguards, including maintained software, multifactor authentication, appropriate access restrictions, and employee awareness. Keep backups protected from unauthorized access and test that they can be restored. These measures help reduce exposure and support recovery, but no individual safeguard eliminates every ransomware risk.

Why is cybersecurity training important for employees?

Employees handle email, account access, customer information, and sensitive requests as part of their work. Practical training helps them recognize suspicious messages, verify unusual instructions, and report concerns. Clear procedures and technical safeguards should reinforce that training so employees understand both what to watch for and how to respond.

What should we do if we suspect a cybersecurity incident?

Contact your IT or security provider through a trusted communication channel and follow your incident response plan. Suspected malware may require isolating affected devices from the network when safe to do so. Preserve relevant messages, alerts, and logs, and coordinate restoration with the team handling the incident.

What does your network security assessment include?

Our assessment reviews antivirus and network security status, Windows and third-party software updates, installed applications, and the health of critical systems. It also includes an inventory of computers and network devices. The findings help identify weaknesses and provide a clearer basis for decisions about your technology and security.
