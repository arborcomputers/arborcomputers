Ann Arbor IT and repair questions, answered

Quick answers about our shop, on-site support and services.

Where can I get computer repair in Ann Arbor?

Arbor Computers at 3201 E. Eisenhower Pkwy, Ann Arbor, MI 48108 repairs Macs, MacBooks, Windows PCs and laptops. Walk-ins are welcome with no appointment needed, Monday to Friday 8 AM to 6 PM and Saturday 8 AM to 3 PM.

Do you provide IT support for businesses and dental offices?

Yes. We provide managed IT, business IT support, dental IT and cybersecurity for offices and dental practices across Southeast Michigan, with on-site visits and remote support.

Do you offer on-site IT support outside Ann Arbor?

Yes. We provide on-site service for homes and businesses across Southeast Michigan, including Ypsilanti, Plymouth, Novi, Troy, Livonia, Dearborn and Detroit, along with remote support.

Can you help remotely?

Yes. Remote support is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week for many software, email and connectivity issues.

Do you build custom PCs?

Yes. We build custom gaming PCs and professional workstations in Ann Arbor, with components chosen for your games, video editing, CAD or development work.
