Custom PC questions, answered

Clear answers to the questions gamers and creators ask us most.

How much does a custom gaming PC cost?

Our four tiers start at $1,500 for Essential, $2,000 for Performance, $3,500 for Enthusiast and $5,000 for Flagship. Every build is tuned to your games, display and budget, with pricing confirmed before anything is ordered.

Do I need to know which parts I want?

No. Tell us your games or apps, display and budget. We choose the components and balance performance, cooling, noise and upgrades for you.

Do you build workstations for video editing and CAD?

Yes. We build professional workstations for video editing, 3D and rendering, design, photography, CAD and development, with CPU, GPU and memory balanced for your software.

Can you build a quiet or compact PC?

Yes. We build small-form-factor and low-noise systems, balancing size and quiet operation with the cooling your components need.

Can you build around parts I already own?

Yes. We can plan upgrades or a new build around parts you already have, checking compatibility, fit and airflow first.

Do you support the PC after I pick it up?

Yes. Every build is assembled, cable-managed, configured and checked before handoff, with local support and upgrades from our Ann Arbor team whenever you need them.
