Computer repair questions, answered

Clear answers to the questions our repair customers ask most.

Do I need an appointment for computer repair?

No. Walk-ins are welcome during shop hours at 3201 E. Eisenhower Pkwy in Ann Arbor. You can also call or send your repair details ahead, so we’re ready for you.

Do you repair Macs and MacBooks?

Yes. We repair MacBook Air and Pro, iMac, Mac mini and Mac Studio, from macOS and startup issues to battery, charging and storage, with diagnostics for your exact model.

Will my files be safe during a repair?

Your files come first. We back up before work where possible, and we discuss data protection and recovery options with you before any repair begins.

Can you remove a virus or malware?

Yes. We investigate pop-ups, suspicious activity and unwanted software, clean up the problem and give you practical advice for protecting your accounts afterward.

Is it worth upgrading an older computer?

Often, yes. SSD and memory upgrades can make an older computer feel fast again. We find what is holding it back, then advise whether to repair, upgrade or replace.

Will you explain the problem before fixing it?

Yes. We find the cause and explain it in plain language first, then you decide. We let you know when it’s ready and walk you through the fix.
