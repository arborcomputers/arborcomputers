Common security questions

Is free antivirus enough for a business?

A business needs protection that is managed centrally, kept current on every device, and watched by someone who can respond when it finds something.

Do we need a VPN if we use Microsoft 365?

A VPN matters when staff reach office files, servers, practice software or other systems that aren’t in the cloud. We’ll recommend what fits how your team works.

Can you make us HIPAA compliant?

We support the technical safeguards behind a HIPAA security program: access controls, endpoint protection, updates, backups and secure remote access. Compliance remains your organization’s program and responsibility.

We think we’ve been hacked. What should we do?

Call us at 734.707.1122. Keep everything in place, change passwords from a device you trust, and we’ll help contain the problem and restore what’s affected.
