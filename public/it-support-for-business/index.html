Business IT questions, answered

Clear answers to the questions businesses ask us most.

What does business IT support include?

Everyday technical support, monitoring and maintenance, security fundamentals, planning and documentation, plus servers, networks, Microsoft 365, file access and backup, all from one accountable Ann Arbor team.

Do you offer onsite and remote support?

Yes. We help at your office or remotely, whichever fits the problem. Many software issues can be handled remotely, while hardware and network faults may need an onsite visit.

Can you support Microsoft 365 and business email?

Yes. We support Microsoft 365 and Exchange Online, business email and shared files across cloud and NAS storage, with email protection and access controls for sensitive data.

How do you protect our business data?

We identify your critical systems, set up local, cloud or hybrid backup with retention and restore access, and test the recovery steps so your team knows what to expect.

Will we need to replace our equipment?

Not necessarily. We start by reviewing your devices, network, accounts and backups, then set clear priorities: what to fix now, what to plan, and what can wait.

How do we get started?

Tell us how your team works and what you need most. We assess your setup, recommend a clear plan and provide ongoing support from a local team that knows your environment.
