Dental IT questions, answered

Clear answers to the questions dental practices ask us most.

Which dental software do you support?

We support practice software environments including Dentrix, Eaglesoft and Open Dental, along with imaging workstations, servers and coordination with your software and imaging vendors.

Do you support every dental specialty?

Yes. We support general dentistry, pediatric dentistry, orthodontics, periodontics, endodontics and oral surgery, with IT planned around how each specialty works.

Do you offer onsite and remote dental IT support?

Yes. Our Ann Arbor team supports dental practices across Southeast Michigan with onsite and remote help for workstations, imaging, software and networks.

Can you make our practice HIPAA compliant?

We provide HIPAA-aware IT planning and the technical safeguards behind it: device and account protection, secure networks, backups and tested recovery plans. Compliance remains your practice’s program, and our work supports it.

How do you protect patient records and imaging from loss?

We design backups around your practice, protect devices and accounts, and test recovery plans, so your team knows how records and imaging will be restored after a problem.

What should I look for in dental IT support?

Look for a local team that knows dental software and imaging, documents your systems, tests backups and recovery, and coordinates with your software and equipment vendors, with onsite and remote help when your practice needs it.

Can you set up IT for a new dental office?

Yes. We plan dental office IT from day one, including infrastructure, workstation and hardware setup, and software and vendor handoffs when you open, move, renovate or add operatories.
